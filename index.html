Verse Display

Say a Bible verse and it appears on the church screen. Works with your church display software such as OBS and EasyWorship.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11. One file, nothing to install.
Download for Windows

Mac

macOS 12 or newer. Pick the one that matches your Mac (see below).
Mac with Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4) Mac with Intel chip
You need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and an internet connection. Verse Display uses their free built-in listening, and it fetches the Bible text online.

How to start

  1. Download the file for your computer.
  2. Open it. A Verse Display window appears. Click Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Say a verse, like "John chapter 3 verse 16", or quote it. The verse is detected and you press Enter to show it.
  4. For the church screen, click Open screen window and drag it to the display, or add the screen address as a Browser source in OBS.

If your computer shows a warning

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

This appears for any new program that is not yet widely downloaded. Click More info, then Run anyway. If nothing opens, right-click the file, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom, then Apply.

Mac: "Verse Display cannot be opened"

Unzip the download and drag Verse Display into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open, then click Open. On newer Macs, if that does not work: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Verse Display. You only do this once.

Which Mac do I have?

Click the Apple menu (top left) → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1 (or M2, M3, M4) choose the Apple chip download. If it says Processor: Intel, choose the Intel download.

About the Bible translations

Many free translations are included, and the church's licensed Bibles (NIV, NKJV, NLT) are already set up inside the program. Nothing to enter. They are for this church's own services only, so please do not share this page or the download with other organisations.
