You need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and an internet connection. Verse Display uses their free built-in listening, and it fetches the Bible text online.

How to start

  1. Download the file for your computer and open it.
  2. A Verse Display window appears. Click Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Say a verse such as "John chapter 3 verse 16", or quote it. The verse is found and you press Enter to show it.
  4. For the church screen click Open screen window and drag it onto the display, or add the screen address as a Browser source in OBS.

If your computer shows a warning

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

This appears for any new program that is not yet widely downloaded. Click More info, then Run anyway. If nothing opens, right-click the file, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom, then Apply.

Mac: "Verse Display Not Opened"

Apple shows this for any app that has not been through its paid approval. The app is safe, and you only do this once. Unzip the download and drag Verse Display into Applications, then:

  1. Double-click it. When the warning appears, click Done (do not click Move to Trash).
  2. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to Verse Display.
  4. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway once more.
Which Mac do I have?

Click the Apple menu (top left), then About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1 (or M2, M3, M4) choose the Apple chip download. If it says Processor: Intel, choose the Intel download.

About the Bible translations

Many free translations are included, and the church's licensed Bibles (NIV, NKJV, NLT) are already set up inside the program, so there is nothing to enter. They are for this church's own services only, so please do not share this page or the download with other organisations.
